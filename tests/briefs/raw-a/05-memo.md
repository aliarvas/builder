This memo replaces the clauses named below. Everything else in CP-7 v3.2 and in my e-mail of 5 Oct stays as it is.

1. Deadline, early band (CP-7 7.2). For customers 1 to 7 days late the deadline is now the 10th business day after today (was the 12th). The late band (8 to 45 days) keeps the 7th business day. Persuasion attempts do not change.

2. Order of the security questions (CP-7 5.1). Ask the last four digits of the phone number on file FIRST, then the date of birth. The date format rules of 5.1 still apply to the date of birth.

Please confirm the pilot reflects this before the next test run.
